**F1_landscape.** The cost of each shape's cheapest feasible flow over area and aspect ratio (13 x 13 samples, 48/6 cells): pumping power per channel at a fixed duty (a), per metre of first wall at a fixed flux (b). Hatched shapes are infeasible: no flow keeps the peaks within 625 K, or the one that does breaks Re/Ha <= 200. The star is the thermal optimum, the ring Stage 2's design.

**F2_cap.** The cost along the area cap, where both optima lie, against the aspect ratio (29 samples, 48/6 cells). At a fixed duty (a) Stage 2's design sits at the minimum; at a fixed flux (b) the minimum is squarer, and Stage 2's flatter design, whose wider wall collects more heat, costs 21 % more per metre of first wall.

**F3_designs.** Each design's outlet cross-section, drawn to scale, from the developed duct at its cheapest feasible flow (48/6 cells). The heated wall z = -b (black) faces the plasma; the field B runs along y; the flow runs along x, out of the page (⊙). Every design's wall peak is 625 K by construction. At a fixed duty the two designs nearly coincide; at a fixed flux the optimum is narrower along the field, with about twice Stage 2's aspect ratio.

**F4_capacity.** At the Stage 2 design's pumping power for 1.5 kW (2.20 mW), the duty each aspect ratio removes within 625 K at the area cap. It peaks near Stage 2's aspect ratio; a square duct removes 0.90 kW. Every sample is laminar (Re/Ha <= 200).
